## Overview

This project studies distributed information gathering when targets are unevenly clustered.
It reformulates regional search as a multi-armed bandit problem so that robots can balance exploration and exploitation without a central controller.

## Method

The system combines distributed Bernoulli Thompson Sampling with PHD-based target estimation and Lloyd control.
Robots share probabilistic information with teammates, while a goal-swapping strategy improves the assignment of search and tracking targets.

## Publication

The work was published in _Frontiers in Plant Science_ in 2025.
