## Overview

A compact gravity energy storage platform was designed to connect mechanical structure,
motion control, energy conversion, and experimental validation.

## Prototype

The prototype is approximately 1510 × 910 × 1630 mm and lifts a 5 kg mass through 1.44 m.
It operates at about 0.2 m/s, stores roughly 70.6 J of potential energy,
and targets 19.6 W output with measured efficiency above 90%.
