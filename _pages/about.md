## About Me

I am **Mingjia Chen (陈铭佳)**, an undergraduate student in Automation at the
[School of Electrical and Automation Engineering, Nanjing Normal University](https://www.nnu.edu.cn/).
My research focuses on autonomous robots operating in partially observable and dynamic environments.
I am particularly interested in how robots can understand a scene, predict future changes,
formulate useful tasks, and improve their decisions through execution feedback.

## Research Interests

- **Embodied AI:** autonomous task formulation, large-language-model planning, semantic memory, and closed-loop execution.
- **Multi-Robot Systems:** distributed perception, dynamic coverage, active gathering, and task allocation.
- **Robot Learning:** reinforcement learning, sim-to-real deployment, legged locomotion, and long-range navigation.

## Education

**B.E. in Automation**, Nanjing Normal University, 2023–Present.

## Current Work

I am currently working on the **CAFE** framework for autonomous task formulation and execution,
dynamic multi-robot coverage planning, and sim-to-real deployment on mobile and quadruped robots.
My work combines semantic memory, prediction, planning, and feedback to improve decision making under uncertainty.
