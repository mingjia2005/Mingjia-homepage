I am **Mingjia Chen (陈铭佳)**, an undergraduate student in Automation at the
[School of Automation, Nanjing Normal University](https://www.nnu.edu.cn/).
My research focuses on building autonomous robots that can understand their surroundings,
formulate useful tasks, and act reliably in complex environments.

## Research Interests

- **Embodied AI:** autonomous task formulation, large-language-model planning, semantic memory, and closed-loop execution.
- **Multi-Robot Systems:** distributed perception, dynamic coverage, active gathering, and task allocation.
- **Robot Learning:** reinforcement learning, sim-to-real deployment, legged locomotion, and long-range navigation.
- **Intelligent Energy Systems:** data-driven modeling and topology representation for power electronics.

## Education

**B.E. in Automation**, Nanjing Normal University, 2023–Present.

## Current Work

I am currently exploring closed-loop embodied agents, multi-robot coordination, and learning-based robot deployment.
My recent work includes the **CAFE** framework for autonomous task formulation and execution,
dynamic multi-robot coverage planning, and navigation experiments on mobile and quadruped robots.
